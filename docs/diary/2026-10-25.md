## What we started with

Two PDFs in a folder called ReadyLab: a deep-research pitch for a project called BoundaryLab, and an audited engineering runbook for the same idea written for Team ODIN. ## What we did

**Read everything and wrote the two foundation documents.** `spec.md` captures the whole project in one place: the falsification formulation (find the world closest to nominal that breaks the policy), the architecture and authority boundaries, the data contracts and status machines, the search and minimization algorithms, the experimental protocol, the release gate semantics, every integration with its smoke test and fallback, the four dashboard surfaces, the gate table G00 to G15, the timeline, and the source register. Every statement is labelled DECIDED, PROPOSED, DOC-VERIFIED, EXECUTED or OPEN so reviewers can see what is fixed and what is ours to change. `CLAUDE.md` is the short version loaded every session: twelve hard rules distilled from the runbook's grounding protocol, the host inventory, the division of labour, and the pre-submission adversarial checklist.

**Named it.** The pitch called it BoundaryLab, the folder said ReadyLab. We chose **Skuld**: the Norn of what shall be in the Völuspá, whose name also means debt. The regression suite is the debt every release must pay. Team ODIN building Skuld is coherent, since Odin is the one who seeks the Norns' knowledge of what is to come. We checked the namesakes (a Norwegian marine insurer, a manga goddess who is, fittingly, the tinkerer) and the handles: `skuld` and `skuld-ai` are taken on GitHub, so the repo is `atcro/skuldai`.
