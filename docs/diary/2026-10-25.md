## What we did

**Read everything and wrote the two foundation documents.** `spec.md` captures the whole project: the falsification formulation (find the world closest to nominal that breaks the policy), architecture and authority boundaries, data contracts, algorithms, experimental protocol, release gate semantics, integrations with smoke tests and fallbacks, the four dashboard surfaces, gates G00 to G15, timeline and sources. Every statement is labelled DECIDED, PROPOSED, DOC-VERIFIED, EXECUTED or OPEN. `CLAUDE.md` is the short version loaded every session: twelve hard rules from the runbook's grounding protocol, host inventory, division of labour, and the pre-submission adversarial checklist.

**Named it.** The pitch said BoundaryLab, the folder said ReadyLab. We chose **Skuld**: the Norn of what shall be, whose name also means debt. The regression suite is the debt every release must pay, and Odin is the one who seeks the Norns' knowledge, so Team ODIN fits. `skuld` and `skuld-ai` are taken on GitHub, so the repo is `atcro/skuldai`.
